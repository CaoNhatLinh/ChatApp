# Call history — member extension

Mode: Operate. Extend Signal Relay inside the existing chat workspace; no new
visual identity, page route, provider claim or shipping raster. Task: recall a
personal/DM call, choose a UTC month, inspect two participant consent facts.

Existing route/state matrix: `call-history-client-verification-2026-10-23.md`.
Scope: own list beside chat filters; DM list in the header's advanced menu even
when starting a call is unavailable. A single protected-focus reading panel
preserves the conversation/composer, rather than nested account-setting modals.
Known authorized DM summaries may supply peer names; otherwise show a neutral
role and actual identifier, never invented profile/deletion/presence data.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Recall a call without leaving the conversation or turning consent into
an invented talk duration. Refuse a metrics dashboard.

OWN-WORLD: Opaque app background, foreground, muted and border tokens; compact
sans, shared buttons, quiet divided rows. Room colors never tint reading text.

STORY: Choose month, scan incoming/outgoing outcomes, open exact participant
facts, return to the list; paging and errors remain explicit.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Compact title and close, month/refresh toolbar, one scrollable
list. Detail replaces that list with back and role/timestamp facts. Mobile is
full-height; desktop capped36rem. Focus follows list/detail transitions, no
entrance choreography or media acquisition.

FORM: Local extension of the incumbent reading plane; no concept seed required.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
