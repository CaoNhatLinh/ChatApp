# Member sticker picker and history

Mode: Operate. Local extension of the existing Signal Relay conversation, not a replacement identity.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Pick from published packs and send; no member catalog management or upload controls.

OWN-WORLD: Existing ink/light surfaces, semantic foreground/border tokens, compact buttons; artwork keeps its own colors and aspect ratio.

STORY: Open stickers, choose a pack, send an image. Loading and recovery stay beside the affected list/message.

FIRST VIEWPORT: An inline shelf above the composer, horizontally browsable pack choices above contained artwork. Close returns focus to the composer. Historical artwork stays inside the message footprint.

FORM: Local extension, no concept seed. Restrained product controls, no ornamental animation. Narrow screens use fewer artwork columns.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
